The apps are not code-signed yet, so your system may show a warning the first time you open Pagelark. Windows: choose "More info", then "Run anyway". macOS: right-click the app, choose "Open", then confirm. Linux: extract the archive and run the notebook_app file. Android: open the file and allow installs from your browser when Android asks.

Made for thinking, not formatting

The things you wish your notes app did, without the extras that get in the way.

Freeform pages

Double-click anywhere to place a text box. Drag, resize and colour it. Pages scroll in every direction.

Images and ink

Paste a screenshot or add a picture, then draw on top of it with the pen. Move and resize everything freely.

Sections and pages

Organise a notebook the way a paper one works, with sections and pages.

Search everything

Find any note across all your pages in a moment with Ctrl/Cmd + F.

Your data, your files

Everything is saved as plain JSON on your computer. Copy a backup or export a page as Markdown whenever you like.

Light and dark

Switch themes with one click, or follow your system.

Free and open source

No account, no ads, no subscription. The code is on GitHub under the MIT licence.
